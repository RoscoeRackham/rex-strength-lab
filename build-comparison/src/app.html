Build Comparison

The score is fat-free mass ÷ height³ × 1.8. That is the FFMI (fat-free mass ÷ height², in kg/m²) the same body would have if it were 1.80 m tall. Body fat is mostly unknown, so every score is a range.

Where the body stops working

    The score, and the natural limit

    The score asks how much fat-free mass a body would carry if it were 1.80 m tall, at the same shape. No hard natural limit is known.
