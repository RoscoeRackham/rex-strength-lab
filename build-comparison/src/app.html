The lineup

Everyone stands at the same height, in score order. Only the build differs.

Each figure stands on its score. The spacing is not to scale; the tape below is. Hatched floor means no build has been measured there yet. Select a figure for its numbers and sources.

How to read a figure

Every figure is a flat shape. Its edge says where the shape came from.

To scale

A log tape: each step to the right multiplies the score. Each bar runs across the build's plausible body fat (and its height and weight where sources disagree); the dot is the working value.

Where the body stops working

    The score and the natural limit

    The score asks how much fat-free mass a body would carry if it were 1.80 m tall at the same shape. No hard natural ceiling is known.

    Measure a character

    Load an A-pose model sheet, type the height, and get the volume, mass, girths and score.
