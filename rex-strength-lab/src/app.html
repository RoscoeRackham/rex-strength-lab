Their powers

All of these are their absorption at work, used on purpose. Each switch suspends one piece of real physics, on purpose. Everything else stays real. Comic-book physics is their default: Unbreakable footing, They take their own recoil, Things keep their form, Safe passenger and Air resistance absorbed are on unless you turn them off (they choose when not to use them), and so is Never knocked back, one switch each. Bolted and the other switches are off by default. (The Collide tab’s two thought experiments, Truly immovable and Light speed, stay on that tab: they are about the test, not their powers.)

Muscle physics
Holding their ground
Bolted to the planet
On, a push along the ground can’t slide his planted feet: it goes into the planet instead. He still walks, runs and jumps. What else it changes depends on the move, and each tab says so: weak ground can still give way under him, and in some two-player tests the ground around his feet can tear out or a very fast hit is over before the bolts can help (Unbreakable footing rules out both). Off, he is just standing, and friction holds him. One each, so a contest can bolt one side and not the other. For their own throws, punches, stomps, pulls, shoves and catches, They take their own recoil keeps them planted too, but it sends the push back to their source, the endless ocean, and with both on that is where it goes. (For a pull it takes only the rope’s pull back: his own steps are the move, so they still push on the ground.)
Never knocked back
On, a hit from outside a contest doesn’t move him: its momentum goes to their source, the endless ocean, even if he is bolted, and the motion it frees as it stops at him is taken back unless he means it (canon; the heat it made in the air on the way stays real). Off, it moves him as real physics says. In their contests (arm wrestling, every Wrestle move, Collide, the partner toss) they let force move: the other’s push on him is real, and only his own push back is taken (They take their own recoil). A catch is his own push too, so that power takes it back. So this switch acts in one case only: a throw that reaches the catcher as a fireball or plasma in the game of catch (Things keep their form off, some of the air’s drag left), which hits him rather than being caught.

Run the record books

Each card names its source and how solid it is. Verified means an official record or published measurement, checked against the source. Canon means the number is printed in the story and was checked in the text. Reported means press or reviews say the story prints it, but the page itself wasn’t seen. Claimed or disputed means it can’t be checked or is contested. Estimate means the source gives no usable number, so the figure is our own reading. Not quantified means the story shows the feat but never gives a number.
