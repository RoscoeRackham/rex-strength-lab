SUMMARY: Growth-mode physics for BRIEF-presence.md, checked against fetched sources and recomputed from the canon figures (3,007 kg, 3.048 m, 1,060 kg/m³, 2.84 m³, equivalent ball radius 0.878 m).

(1) Worked examples. The speed of light is exact (NIST CODATA 2022). The IAU 2015 solar luminosity of 3.828e26 W is confirmed from the PDF. For Chicxulub, Morgan et al. 2022 (Nat. Rev. Earth Environ.; NSF PAR copy) gives "about 10^23 J" in the abstract and "about 5 × 10^23 J" from the transient crater, so the brief's 4.2e23 J lies inside that range. For Krakatoa, the best primary figure is Harkrider & Press 1967: 100–150 Mt (4.2–6.3e17 J) as an air-wave equivalent. Total-energy estimates range from 1e17 to 4.6e18 J; the popular 200 Mt figure is weakly sourced.
- Every worked number in the brief checks out to 2 significant figures, with one exception: Chicxulub at pure density is 1.65e6 kg/m³, which rounds to 1.6e6, not the brief's 1.7e6.
- Krakatoa adds 9 kg (1–51 kg across all estimates), which is unnoticeable.
- Chicxulub adds 4,700 t (×1,555): mix 10.4 m and 41,800 kg/m³; pure size 35 m.
- One second of the Sun's output adds 4.3e6 t: mix 32 m and 1.3e6 kg/m³; pure size 342 m; pure density 1.5e9 kg/m³.

(2) Gradual growth. A compact body whose volume changes radiates a pressure wave p = ρ0·V̈/(4πr) (Rienstra & Hirschberg, Curle's theorem). Standing on the ground doubles it (same text). The US Bureau of Mines (RI 8485) gives 134 dB, about 100 Pa, as the safe limit for houses, with rattle complaints above about 120 dB (20 Pa). OSHA allows robots 250 mm/s, a speed "slow enough for a person to avoid hazardous contact"; comfortable walking is 1.3–1.5 m/s (Bohannon 1997). With a smooth rate profile, the air pulse needs only about 2 s (Chicxulub, pure size) to 22 s (one solar second, pure size). The shove sets the real limit: about 1.5 min (Chicxulub, mix), 4 min (Chicxulub, pure size), 14 min (Sun, mix) and 43 min (Sun, pure size). Pure density has neither limit.

(3) Instant growth. The chosen time is the final height ÷ 1,540 m/s (sound speed in soft tissue, from review-level PMC sources). Big growth then moves his sides at up to 444 m/s (Mach 1.3) and his head at up to 1,540 m/s. We solved Taylor's 1946 expanding-sphere problem ourselves and checked it with an independent hydro code (agreement within 1%). The result: the surface overpressure is about ρ0u² (within 2% above 0.8 times the speed of sound). Courant & Friedrichs reproduce Taylor's curve; the printed sphere-speed curve sits 0.04–0.05 c0 above ours. The flat-piston relation from the NASA normal-shock equations is the upper bound and matches the existing Collins relation. Baker et al. 1977 (NASA CR-134906) supply two rules: rigid ground doubles the equivalent energy (2E), and beyond R̄ ≈ 2 a high-explosive curve of equal energy is an upper limit. Instant Chicxulub growth at pure size is therefore 2–3 atm at his surface and 0.9–1.5e9 J into the air, like 210–350 kg of TNT. Windows break out to about 100–120 m.

(4) Runaway loop. Pushing air returns p/(ρc²): about 1e-15 for pure size (3–5e-15 if instant), 1e-16 to 1e-18 for mix, and 0 for pure density. Rock returns at most about 4e-11, and any ordinary matter at most about 1.5e-8. For their own gravity, the Newtonian ratio d(3GM²/5R)/d(Mc²) is 0.6, 0.55 or 0.5 times r_s/R (pure density, mix, pure size). It would reach 1 only at 0.6, 0.55 or 0.5 r_s, which is inside the event horizon. The relativistic uniform ball (Lattimer & Prakash 2001, eq. 34) 

MODEL: MASS AND FORM. Δm = E/c², with c = 299,792,458 m/s exact. k = 1 + Δm/3007. Volume V = V0·k^a, with V0 = 2.8368 m³ and a = 1 for pure size, 0.5 for mix (the default), 0 for pure density. A slider for a between 0 and 1 is fine. Height H = 3.048·(V/V0)^(1/3). Equivalent radius R = 0.8782·(V/V0)^(1/3). Density ρ = 3007k/V. Feed m = 3007k into the Weight tab. That gravitational mass rises by exactly E/c²; never add the loop's extra rest mass to it. Endless ocean: show the energy absorbed, with zero mass change. Label it canon.

GRADUAL GROWTH. Profile: fraction of mass added s(t) = t/T − sin(2πt/T)/(2π), so the rate goes as 1 − cos(2πt/T). This is a choice; label it.
- Sample s once per (form, k) to get k_V = max|V̈|·T²/ΔV (exactly 2π for pure size; 14–55 for mix) and k_H = max(dH/dt)·T/ΔH (about 1.9–2 for pure size; 2.6–7 for mix). Neither depends on T.
- Air pulse at a bystander d metres from his final surface, on rigid ground: p = ρ0·k_V·ΔV / (2π·r·T²), with r = R_final + d and ρ0 = 1.225 kg/m³.
- Pressure limit: T_p = √(ρ0·k_V·ΔV / (2π·r·p_safe)). Default p_safe = 100 Pa (USBM safe level). Offer 20 Pa ("nothing rattles"), 3.4 kPa (windows) and 34.5 kPa (eardrums) as the higher presets.
- Shove limit: T_s = k_H·ΔH / u_safe, with u_safe = 0.25 m/s by default (OSHA) and 1.4 m/s (walking) as an option. Use ΔR instead of ΔH for people beside him.
- Shortest safe duration = max(T_p, T_s). Say which limit sets it; it is almost always the shove.
- Valid while c0·T is much greater than H and the surface speed is far below 340 m/s.
- Ignores wind, buildings, terrain and anyone pinned against a wall (crushed at any speed). Pure density has no air or shove limit; show the Weight-tab hazards instead.

INSTANT GROWTH. t_x = H_final / 1540 m/s; this is an estimate; say so. Side speed u = ΔR/t_x; head speed ΔH/t_x.
- Surface overpressure is a range.
  - Low end: the expanding-sphere value. Use the table k(u/c0) × ρ0u²: k = 1.16, 1.08, 1.04, 1.02, 1.01 and 1.00 at u/c0 = 0.3, 0.45, 0.6, 0.8, 1.0 and ≥ 1.5.
  - High end: the flat piston. M = (γ+1)u/(4c0) + √(((γ+1)u/(4c0))² + 1); Δp = p0·(2γ/(γ+1))·(M² − 1), with γ = 1.4, c0 = 340.29 m/s, p0 = 101,325 Pa.
  - For small u this tends to ρ0·c0·u (a thump, not a blast).
- Excess energy into the air: E_x = Δp·ΔV. Report p0·ΔV separately as "atmosphere pushed back", not blast.
- TNT mass W = E_x / 4.184e6 J/kg. On hard ground W_free = 2W. Then use the engine's Kinney–Graham curve for how far windows, eardrums and so on are affected.
- Kinney–Graham is an upper limit only beyond R̄ = r·(p0/(2E_x))^(1/3) > 2. Closer in, cap the pressure at the surface Δp.
- Anchor the growth at his soles, so no ground is driven into.
- State that zero duration would need infinite energy (Δp grows as u²).

RUNAWAY LOOP.
- Air: f_air = (p0 + Δp)·ΔV / (Δm·c²), which is 0 for pure density.
- Own gravity: Newtonian f_grav = 0.3·(2 − α)·r_s/R, with α = a/3. Or use relativity: f = 1 − dM/dM_p, with M_p = M(1 + g(β)), g from Lattimer & Prakash eq. 34, and β = GM/(Rc²).
- Ground: about 0 when anchored at the soles.
- Show f as a plain power of ten ("about 1 part in 10^15") and the multiplier 1/(1 − f).
- Show where it crosses 1: only when R ≤ r_s, i.e. the Weight tab's black-hole state (for canon pure density, 5.9e26 kg, which takes 5.3e43 J).
- Half-life: apply it only when f ≥ 1 (isBlackHole after growth). No physics sets its value, so label any default a story choice.
- Bondi infall: per kg it releases GM/R = β·c² (1 − √(1 − 2β) in relativity). It only runs when r_B > R, which needs density above about 5e14 kg/m³ at canon size. Cap its total at the atmosphere's mass (5.1e18 kg).

REC: 1. Instant growth: anchoring at the soles contradicts the blast model. The recommendation anchors growth at the soles but sizes the blast with a ball expanding about its centre. With sole anchoring, upward-moving surfaces sweep exactly 1/3 of the added volume at up to the head speed (Mach 3-4.5). Because Δp grows as u², energy into the air is about 2× (ball anchored at its base) to 4.3× (Rex-shaped column) the ball value. The local top pressure is about 2.4-2.9 MPa, not 2-3 atm. Either widen E_x, TNT and reach (reach ×1.3-1.6), or state that the ball model assumes growth about the centre.

2. Instant growth: momentum is missing (canon: momentum is never absorbed). With the soles fixed, the centre of mass rises at about half the head speed: about 700 m/s for Chicxulub pure size, a momentum of 3.3e9 kg·m/s. The ground must push 3.9e11 N, 4.7 GPa on Rex's grown 83 m² soles (twice that for Ollie's boots), which exceeds any rock strength in the data. Nothing can then pull him back, so he would be launched about 25 km up unless 'bolted to the planet' is on. The alternative, anchoring at the centre of mass, drives his feet about ΔH/2 into the ground at about 700 m/s. Gradual growth needs a matching lift-off check: T ≥ about √(πΔH/g), about 3 s for Chicxulub pure size. It is never binding next to the shove limit, but it should be computed. 'Ground ≈ 0' is right for the loop ratio only, not for the hazard.

3. Windows: use the existing 3.4-6.9 kPa band, not 6.9 kPa alone (100-220 m for Chicxulub pure size).

4. The 20 Pa preset uses the wrong measurement basis and the wrong label. USBM's 120 dB is on the 6 Hz high-pass meter; on the 0.1 Hz basis it is about 125 dB, about 36 Pa. A seconds-long growth pulse barely registers on a 6 Hz meter, and the source says rattling still occurs below the safe level, so 'nothing rattles' is false. It is also a stricter preset, not a 'higher' one. The window (3.4 kPa) and eardrum (34.5 kPa) presets are fast-rising blast thresholds and are not validated for a smooth pulse lasting seconds; label them that way.
