==== hint: They take their own recoil: the push back from their own throws, punches, stomps, pulls and shoves, and from stopping what they catch, goes into their source, the endless ocean, not into the planet. So they aren’t shoved back, launched or carried off, and none of it reaches the ground, so it doesn’t shake the ground. The endless ocean takes the whole push back of the move, the other way: the momentum of what they set moving or stop, plus whatever resists it while they push (a pulled load’s rolling, a shoved partner’s feet). A punch into air leaves none: when the arm stops, the fist’s momentum comes back. A jump or a sprint start is the move itself, so it is unchanged. Being pushed by someone else (the two-player contests, Collide) is still what Bolted is for.
