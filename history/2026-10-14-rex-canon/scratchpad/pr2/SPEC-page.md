## The engine as built (commit 87c9dc3), from its author's report

New figures, present only when the named power is on (never `{J:0}`):
- `recoilAbsorbed { J, T, thetaDeg }` on throwObject (and `throwPartner(...).r`), stomp, punch, pull, shove and armWrestle (the pin). J in kg·m/s, ≥ 0, Infinity when unbounded. T is the push's own time (push.t; t10m for the pull; the swing time for the pin). thetaDeg is the angle of HIS push above level; the absorbed momentum points the opposite way (throw: back along the throw line; punch and shove: 0, backward; pull: 0 is the way he walks, the push back points toward the load; stomp and pin: −90, the push back points straight up). The punch's carries `returned: true` (net zero over the punch).
- `recoilAbsorbedT { J, T, thetaDeg }` on catchLeg (the thrower) and tossPartner (90).
- `recoilAbsorbedC { J, T, angleDeg }` on catchLeg and tossPartner: angleDeg is how steeply it arrives (0 level); the absorbed momentum points along its arrival. T the stroke time.
- `toGround.downTaken` only where Unbreakable footing's cap applies.
- The catcher with his power on: `k.R` Infinity, slide and uAfter 0, slips and carried false. Every catch outcome where the object reaches him.
- With the power on the foot load is (body.m + obj.m) g in throwOnce, catchIt and relCatch (sinkRatio/sinkRatioRaw): his throw can't break the ground; the ground hazard and 'break the ground' preset become never where only his recoil broke it (car at 45°, 10⁹ kg: 650,000 kg-force → never).
