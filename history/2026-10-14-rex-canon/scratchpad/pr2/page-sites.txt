The work falls into five groups:

1. **Plain rewording.** About 20 page sentences, chips, tiles and math lines, plus the #sw-recoil hint, change from "into the planet" to "the endless ocean" (their source). This also covers the eyebrow, suppliedText, five cards and seven README paragraphs.

2. **Pull's shaking text.** The Pull tab works out its own shaking (app.html:3315) without reading the engine. Dropping MOVES.pull.shake in the engine does NOT remove the "shakes the ground" item, so the tab must change too. The effort-box guard `move === 'pull' && !!P.RESTRAINT.MOVES.pull.shake` (app.html:3462) would also fall through to the wrong generic text ("doesn't model"). Pull should say why it has no shaking the way Punch does: through noShakeWhy, which feeds the ceiling line and the plaster/rattle notes.

3. **New credits.** The effort box (offBy, app.html:3627-3629) needs a plaster credit for They take their own recoil on Throw. The Catch tab needs a new credit for the catcher's stop (Q5), with a matching chip and tile.

4. **How the engine reports these figures.** It must leave toGround, toGroundT and toGroundC undefined when the power is on, not set them to zero. The page treats any object as truthy: groundShake({J:0}) returns {none:true}, and items then print "Rex's feet hand the ground the throw's push (0 kg·m/s …)". It must keep RESTRAINT.shakeOff exported, because the page's noShake calls it for throw, jump and stomp.

5. **Tests that will break.** test/shots.js:480 checks the chip text "His recoil goes into the planet: their power". test/shots.js:524-531 expects the plaster preset to find an effort on the Wrestle shove and on Catch. I ran the engine and confirmed that with the default powers those two crack plaster only through the shover's or thrower's own push, so after the change both become "never". The powers-off page comparison (shots.js:200, s4-catch) needs duoNoModel's shakeOnly sentence kept word for word.

- The Game of catch card and README:115 are missing from its list.
