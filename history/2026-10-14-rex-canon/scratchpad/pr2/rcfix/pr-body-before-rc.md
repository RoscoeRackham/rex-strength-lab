## Decisions the brief left open (README, decisions 1–14; for the review rounds and for you)

2. **His feet carry only the weights.** Holding a load or a partner up is weight, not recoil. As a result, a throw can no longer break the ground under him with the power on, unless the weights alone do it. The throw's break-the-ground preset becomes "never" in those cases (a car at 45° at a 10⁹ kg dial was 650,000 kg-force). 3. **The catch is absorbed in every outcome where the object reaches him.** That covers caught, slams, wreckage and can't-hold-it. Planted, his arms must stop the whole speed.
