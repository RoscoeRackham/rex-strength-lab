## Decisions (record each in the PR body and in the README; the review rounds check them)

1. **Where the push goes.** With `ownRecoil` on, the push back from their own moves goes to the endless ocean (their source): no `toGround` from it, no shaking from it, no weak-ground cap on it. They stay planted (no slide, lurch or hop): `ownAnchor` keeps returning `'bolted'` for the footing of his own push. Report the momentum taken back as a new figure, only when `ownRecoil` is on.
2. **They take their own recoil wins over Bolted to the planet for their own moves.** With both on, his own push still goes to his source, not through the bolts. Bolted stays the setting for being pushed by someone else (and, with the power off, the bolts take his own push as today). Canon: "the push back from their own moves goes into their source, not into the planet".
3. **His feet carry only the weights.** With the power on, the load on his feet (`Nfeet`, `sinkRatio`, `sinkRatioRaw` in throwOnce; the same in throwPartner, the catch's stop and the partner toss) counts his weight and the held load's weight, not the push back. Holding a load or partner up is not recoil (the brief's reading for holding a partner up; the weight rests on the ground through him, as on the Lift tab). In throwOnce: `Nfeet = anc.N(RB)` (= (body.m + obj.m) g) instead of `anc.N(Fload)` when `ctx.ownRecoil`. Consequence: a throw can no longer break the ground under him with the power on (only the weights could), so the throw's ground hazard and its 'break the ground' preset become 'never' where only his recoil broke it. The page must say why (the push back goes to their source; turn the power off to send it into the ground). The brief's "every preset is unchanged" assumed otherwise; record the change.
4. **The catch (Q5, CLAUDE.md "A catch is absorbed (he is never carried off, and nothing goes into the ground) unless he chooses otherwise").** With the catcher's `ownRecoil` on, every catch outcome where the object reaches him (caught, slams, wreckage, cant-hold-it, and relCatch past 1% of light speed) leaves him planted: no slip, `uAfter` 0, slide 0, never carried off, nothing into the ground (`toGroundC` absent), and the whole stop goes to his source (`recoilAbsorbedC`). His arms must then stop the full arrival speed, so some catches turn from caught to slams (for example a bus thrown hard 10 m apart at the default dial) and the energy freed at the catch rises a little: these are consequences, report them. With the power off: today's physics, unchanged. The fireball's push on the catcher (`fireballAt`) is NOT changed here: it is Never knocked back's case (item 4, pull request 4).
5. **Stomp.** With the power on, the upward push on his body (equal to the foot's momentum `push.pB`, since he is held) goes to his source: report it; the foot's blow on the ground stays (it is the move); nothing cancels it, so the page drops the "upper bound" caveat. Engine numbers otherwise unchanged.
6. **Punch.** With the power on the push back goes to his source during the stroke and comes back when his arm stops: net zero in the ocean and the ground. Engine numbers unchanged. (Optionally report `recoilAbsorbed` with a `returned: true` marker; keep it consistent with the ledger.)

## The ledger test (item 3), new section in test/powers.js

For each move (a throw of a car at 0°, 45° and 90°; a stomp; a punch; a clap; a jump; a pull; a shove; the wrestle throw; a catch leg (thrower's push and the catcher's stop as two events); the partner toss; a Collide splat and bounce; instant growth), at three dials (90,718.474 kg-force, the page default; 10⁹ kg; 10¹² kg; growth at E = 10⁶, 10¹⁵ and 4.2 × 10²³ J), with the powers on (all four), off, and Unbreakable only and own recoil only where they differ: add up the momentum along each axis (x along the push, y up) at the end of the push or contact event: the object, his body, the ground, and the ocean's entries, each labelled with its canon case (`recoil`, `supplied` (the ground's share it can't bear), `air`, `growthLift`). The total must be zero to a part in a million of the largest term. And assert the BUCKETS, not just the sum (a mutant that books the recoil in the ground would still close): with own recoil on, no recoil reaches the ground and every ocean entry is a named case; with every power off, no ocean entry except canon's growth lift (instantGrowth reports `Pocean` with every power off: exempt `growthLift`).

- With the powers off the engine doesn't report the ground's share for most moves, and you may not add keys to powers-off results. Derive it in the test from primary figures (`push.pA`, `push.pB`, `mB`, `armMass`, `anchorCapacity`), or add a pure exported helper that is NOT stored in results. Don't make the closure tautological: the reported figures (`toGround`, `recoilAbsorbed`, ...) must be checked against what the push implies.
- Use the exact relativistic momentum where speeds round near c (`p = sqrt(E² + 2 E m c²) / c`).
- Unbounded pushes (J = Infinity): check "unbounded on both sides" instead of a sum.
- Flights: the absorbed share of the air's drag is a pair of equal and opposite impulses both taken back (the object isn't slowed and the air isn't pushed), so it adds nothing net; keep the ledger to push and contact events, and say so in a comment. Don't add drag components unless you need them.
- The stomp's `blow` adds a stamp effective-mass term: use `push.pB` for the foot's momentum.
- weakGround's scalar `supplied` is suppliedDown + suppliedSide (perpendicular parts): use the components.
- The game of catch's `aim.vL` is interpolated: use the exact throw's momentum.
- Collide's not-held bounce with Unbreakable footing over-counts the ground by about 1.1% (it books the splat's footing share while its after-speeds ignore footing). - A powers-off bolted catcher in a slam gets `uAfter > 0` with slide 0 (pre-existing): count `(M + m) uAfter` as the catcher's own momentum in the powers-off ledger; don't change powers-off results.
