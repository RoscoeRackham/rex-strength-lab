=== stomp9  
V: No physical answer here, and the page isn’t broken: this is the canon’s ruling. Ollie takes his own recoil, so nothing can slow him: the push back from his hit goes to his source. Ollie comes down on him from above, and the ground under Rex’s feet holds him up. Unbreakable footing never lets it give: whatever push it can’t bear, their power supplies, so nothing limits that hold. One of them has to give, and neither can, so physics has no answer.

E: All-out. Everything they have.Courtesy ceiling: none. Nothing in this test does harm, even all-out, with the nearest people and buildings 5 m away.
T: Charge speed166 m/sfalling back after a stomp-launchOllie slowsneverhis own recoil goes to his sourceRex movesneverheld without limit: the ground under him, held by their power (Unbreakable footing)
H: Canon (the rulings of 14 Oct 2026): “A charger’s push back from his own hit is his own recoil: it goes to his source, so he doesn’t slow. Where the one he hits is held without limit (never knocked back, or held up by their powers), the collision has no physical answer.” Collide is a contest, so Never knocked back is set aside here; what holds Rex without limit is the ground their power holds up under him.Why neither can give. Ollie could slow only by keeping the push back from the hit, and his goes to his source. Rex could move only if what holds him gave, and landed on from above, he can only go down into the ground, and Unbreakable footing supplies whatever push the ground can’t bear (canon’s case “the ground’s share it can’t bear”). This is the old paradox in the lab’s terms: an unstoppable force (a charger who can’t slow) meets an immovable object (a body their powers hold without limit). Momentum conservation can’t share out a hit that neither side can take, so the two settings can’t both hold.With Unbreakable footing off (or the Break the ground preset), the ground is real and gives: Rex is driven 3.65 m into the concrete slab.With They take their own recoil off, Ollie slows as real physics says, and the hit has an answer: Rex doesn’t budge, and Ollie stops dead.The take-off goes into the ground: his stomp-launch’s foot, 630,000 kg·m/s over 64 ms, a body width from Rex (his Unbreakable footing, as on the Stomp tab). Of that push (630,000 kg·m/s), the ground takes only what it can bear, 24,000 kg·m/s; their power supplies the other 610,000 kg·m/s (canon: one of the few cases where momentum is absorbed; it goes to the endless ocean). That shakes the ground as real physics says: enough that plaster may crack out to 1.75 m, and felt out to 150 m.
