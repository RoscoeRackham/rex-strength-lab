=== fb-on  
V: At 2.36% of light speed the baseball turns to plasma at once. Ollie, 10 m away, is inside the fireball: it reaches him still carrying 3.21 TJ of motion plus 422 GJ of heat. Never knocked back: it doesn’t move Ollie. Its momentum (1 million kg·m/s) goes to the endless ocean, and stopping it frees 3.63 TJ where he stands.
S: Fireball, no ball arrives
E: All-out. Everything they have.Courtesy ceiling: lifting 42.6 t. Past that, the baseball is destroyed.At this effort the baseball is destroyed
T: 
H: His hand still meets real air (their own bodies do): it breaks the sound barrier as he lets go, so the boom stays, and over the end of the throw it drives about 143 g of air ahead of it, up to 1.7 kt TNT of blast at his hand (an upper bound: the hand speeds up through the throw). The effort and courtesy ceiling count it.They take their own recoil: the push back from the throw (1 million kg·m/s) goes into their source, the endless ocean, so none of it reaches the ground and Rex stays put (without it he would be shoved back at 342 m/s).They take their own recoil doesn’t cover this: Ollie doesn’t catch it, it hits him, and an incoming hit isn’t his own push back. That is Never knocked back’s case, and Ollie’s switch is on (Their powers): he isn’t moved, and its momentum goes to the endless ocean. With it off, it would carry him off.Past 1% of light speed the air can’t get out of the way. The baseball keeps its momentum while it scoops up air; by about 55.6 m half its energy has turned to heat (it sweeps air at 50% of the real rate: the share of the drag that still acts).
H: Stopping it at Ollie frees 3.63 TJ (870 t TNT) where he stands. 422 GJ of it is heat already made in the air on the way: that is real. The rest, 3.21 TJ, is its motion, freed as it stops at him: canon takes a hit’s freed energy back by default (it shows as his shimmer, which the lab doesn’t draw yet), so that part goes to the endless ocean, unless he means it (a blast preset). He isn’t moved, so nothing rides on with him.
