H: Canon (the rulings of 14 Oct 2026): “A charger’s push back from his own hit is his own recoil: it goes to his source, so he doesn’t slow. Where the one he hits is held without limit (never knocked back, or held up by their powers), the collision has no physical answer.” Collide is a contest, so Never knocked back is set aside here; what holds Rex without limit is his bolts on ground their power holds.Why neither can give. Ollie could slow only by keeping the push back from the hit, and his goes to his source. Rex could move only if what holds him gave, and his bolts are fixed to the planet: carrying him would mean carrying the ground, and Unbreakable footing supplies whatever push the ground can’t bear (canon’s case “the ground’s share it can’t bear”). This is the old paradox in the lab’s terms: an unstoppable force (a charger who can’t slow) meets an immovable object (a body their powers hold without limit). Momentum conservation can’t share out a hit that neither side can take, so the two settings can’t both hold.With They take their own recoil off, Ollie slows as real physics says, and the hit has an answer: Rex doesn’t budge, and Ollie stops dead.At light speed the air ahead of Ollie can’t get out of the way: it’s shock-heated into plasma on the way in. That much is real; the hit itself has no answer.
