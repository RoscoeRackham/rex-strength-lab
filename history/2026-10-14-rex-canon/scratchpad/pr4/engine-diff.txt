    Pull request 4 of BRIEF-canon.md, engine: contests and Never knocked back
    
    The engine part of item 4 (SPEC decisions 1-11 and 13). Every new branch runs
    only with They take their own recoil on (or, for the fireball, the catcher's
    noKnockback), so with every power off every engine result is unchanged (the
    15,533 powers-off cases in test/powers.js part 1). noKnockback is not in
    anyPower.
    
    - Collide's charge: a charger who takes his own recoil doesn't slow. The one
      hit is carried along at his speed (splat) or sent off at 2v/(1+v^2/c^2)
      (bounce); the charger's source supplies the one hit's gain plus his footing's
      share ('carried', carry { v, vB, pGain, footJ, supplied, stop, ... }). Held
      without limit by their powers (bolted on Unbreakable footing; landed on from
      above where it holds him up): 'paradox' with unbounded: true. Bolted on real
      rock: carried with the rock torn out. The hit's freed energy is absorbed by
      default (hitAbsorbed) and real with o.meanIt. The Truly immovable thought
      experiment is unchanged. Knocked back in a bounce, the footing no longer books
      a ground share its speeds ignore (pull request 2's Known limit).
    - The steady push: the pusher's own footing doesn't cap his push; one-sided
      paradox and rockGives; no rock dipole for a pusher who takes his own recoil.
    - The shoving match: each pushes with the effort he uses: 'bothShoved' (with a
      motion model over the shove's stroke), 'walkOut' at the Pull tab's pace,
      'shovedOff' for a bolted winner. Both at once: each shoved by the other's
      shove.
    - Arm wrestling: reading B (each pad, or standing each one's feet, holds the
      other's press); the brief's reading A leaves the other's real press with
      nothing on his side carrying it, so it is physically incoherent. A pin never
      breaks the table with the power on; standing, his own press isn't capped by
      his own footing.
    - Never knocked back: the fireball's push in the game of catch doesn't move the
      catcher; its momentum goes to the endless ocean (knockAbsorbed), bolted too;
      the air's heat is real, the motion freed at him taken back unless meant.
