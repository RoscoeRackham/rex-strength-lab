## Canon (CLAUDE.md, "Rulings of 14 Oct 2026")

- Never knocked back is a default power for each of them, with a switch each, both on by default. Incoming hits are a charge, a steady push, a shove, the shoving match, being lifted or thrown by the other, a caught object and a partner landing on him. A hit's freed energy is absorbed by default (it shows in his shimmer) and is real only when he means it. A catch is absorbed (he is never carried off, and nothing goes into the ground) unless he chooses otherwise.
- In contests, force moves, so Never knocked back is set aside. Contests are arm wrestling, every Wrestle move, Collide's charge and steady push, and the partner toss. In a contest the other's push is real, and only the push each puts on himself is absorbed (his own recoil).
- A charger's push back from his own hit is his own recoil: it goes to his source, so he doesn't slow. Where the one he hits is held without limit (never knocked back, or held up by their powers), the collision has no physical answer.

## Decisions (record each in a README section for this pull request and in the PR body; the review rounds check them)

### Collide, the charge (engine `COLLIDE.collide`)
1. **The charger of unbounded mass.** With the charger's `ownRecoil` on (and the one hit not the 'truly immovable' thought experiment), he doesn't slow. Use the brief's 'after Q13' sums (`research/brief-canon/runs.js` lines 123–136): in his frame the one hit arrives at his speed v and meets an immovable body, so `h' = headOn(vToMomentum(v, mB), mB, Infinity)`; the one hit leaves at v (splat) or at `2v / (1 + v²/c²)` (bounce: c·tanh(2w), w = atanh(v/c)), with ground-frame momentum γ m v' (bounce: 2γ²m v). The charger's source supplies that gain plus what the one hit's footing hands the ground during the contact (cap the footing's force at the contact force). Report `carry: { v, pGain, footJ, supplied }` and the hit's freed energy Q = h'.Q. Guard against an infinite p (light speed keeps main's 'unstoppable').
2. **How long he is carried.** An unbounded charger never slows, so the carry would last as long as he pushes. The lab ends it at the end of the squash: the charger stops himself there (his own push; its push back goes to his source). The one hit then slides from v on his footing (or sinks, from above). State it as a limit.
3. **The paradox.** Where the one hit is held without limit by their powers: Bolted on Unbreakable footing (sideways), or landed on from above with Unbreakable footing (or Comic ground) holding him up. Test `holdsGround(cB) && (cB.anchor === 'bolted' || !horiz)`. Outcome `'paradox'`, no h, no contact, no toGround; keep `out.speed`. The page explains it FIRST (before anything else on the Collide tab), so a reader doesn't take it for a broken page: X takes his own recoil, so nothing can slow him; Y is held without limit by their powers; one of them has to give and neither can, so physics has no answer (the canon's ruling). Bolted on REAL rock is not held without limit: the rock tears (groundTear) and Y is carried with a plug of rock (`carry.rockGives`), sliding on his own friction.
4. **The 'Truly immovable' thought experiment stays as it is**: it sets the mover's own recoil aside (keyed on `cB.immovable`), so its results equal main's; the tab says the thought experiments are about the test, not their powers (it already does), plus one sentence that the charger's own recoil is set aside there.
5. **The hit's energy.** With the charger's power on, the energy the hit frees (the splat's Q) is absorbed by default and shows only as his shimmer (pull request 5); it is real only when he means it: when the reader picks a blast preset (rattle by air, windows, houses, block), the brief's reading of "when he means it" (confirm in review). The one hit's own air past 1% of light speed and the charger's run-up air are their bodies meeting real air: real.
