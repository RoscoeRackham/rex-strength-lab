### reasoning
Reproduced. For a fireball formed before it reaches the catcher ('before'), the verdict gives a blast 'carrying up to 3.44 TJ' and then 'stopping it frees 3.63 TJ'. It points to 'below' for the motion that is taken back, but catchEnvItems skips its 'Stopping it at … frees …' split when fb.kind === 'before'. Nothing below gives the 192 GJ. Only the blast caption and the math repeat, without a number, that the motion is taken back. The two figures on the view are never reconciled.

### reproduction
1. `node catchall.js ../repo/rex-strength-lab/dist/index.html '{"duoOpen":true,"duoAction":"catch","cObj":"baseball","cStyle":"hard","logKg":14,"keepForm":false,"airAbsorb":0.5,"cLogD":3}'`, then grep for 192, 'motion' and 'below'. Matches:
   - the verdict: "…a blast carrying up to 3.44 TJ, the heat made in the air (its motion as it reaches him is taken back: below)… stopping it frees 3.63 TJ where he stands."
   - the caption: "(the fireball’s heat; its motion at Ollie is taken back)"
   - the math line, with no figure
   - no 192 GJ anywhere
2. Engine `P.catchLeg(rex, ollie{noKnockback}, baseball, 1000, 'hard')`: kind 'before', Ehere 3.633e12, Egap 3.442e12, Earr 1.918e11, knockAbsorbed.E 1.918e11.
3. In src/app.html line 2992, `if (fb.kind !== 'before' && isFinite(fb.Ehere))` gates the split.

### better_fix
In catchEnvItems, change the gate to `if ((fb.kind !== 'before' || fb.knockAbsorbed) && isFinite(fb.Ehere))`, so the Never knocked back split ('…3.44 TJ of it is heat already made in the air on the way: that is real. The rest, 192 GJ, is its motion…') shows for 'before' too. Or say the 192 GJ in the verdict and drop 'below'.
