### reasoning
Reproduced. With every power off, the full Collide text differs between pull request 3's page and this one, in the charge view and in the steady push view. The four-cases grids have new row and column labels and new cell text. The PR body's 'with the powers off the page is the same in 43 of 44 states. Its likely basis is the fixture fields (verdict, chips, tiles, effort, happens, callouts, math), which leave out the grid. Decision 16 does mention the grid relabel, so the owner can find it, but the test claim is overstated. Results (verdicts, chips, tiles) are unchanged.
