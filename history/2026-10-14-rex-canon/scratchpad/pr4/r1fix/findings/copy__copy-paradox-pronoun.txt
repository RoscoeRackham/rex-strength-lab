### reasoning
The text is as quoted, but this is a style point, not a defect. Just above the verdict the selected controls read 'Ollie charges, Rex holds' and 'Jump onto him'. 'Ollie comes down on him' can't refer to Ollie (that would be 'himself'), and Rex is named in the same clause ('the ground under Rex’s feet'). A general reader isn't misled about who lands on whom. The suggested rewording reads a little better, but the brief's standard skips style preferences.
