## severity

## title
Arm wrestling standing, power on: the rock around a bolted loser's bolts is tested against the winner's press, not the press that reaches him

## location
rex-strength-lab/src/engine.js:1842-1843 (out.tearA/out.tearB = (ownRecoil ? the other's press : his own) / groundTear)

## evidence
Page: Arm wrestle with Things keep their form off, Unbreakable footing off, Ollie bolted, Rex's dial at 10⁶ kg and Ollie's own strength at 100 short tons, all-out. Verdict: 'That beats Ollie's arm (224 kN): he pins Ollie in 6.9 ms.' Status: 'Ground around Ollie's bolts tears out'. Item: 'past about 697 kN it breaks up around Ollie's feet.' Engine: armWrestle gives outcome 'pin', contact 222,411 N, tearB 3.52 (= 2.45 MN / 697 kN). In the pin model the loser's arm resists with Pl (run(s, M, Pl)), and loserSlides uses Rl < Pl. The force that reaches Ollie's body and bolts is his own folding arm's 222 kN, which gives tearB 0.32 (no tear), as #34's page had it (tearB 0.319). The engine-mutant E16, which changes this line, survives powers.js and the full shots.js.

## expected
In a pin both bodies carry the loser's press (the engine's contact). In a stalemate each carries the other's equal press. A bolted loser's rock is loaded with 222 kN here and doesn't tear.

## fix
With the power on, use the press that reaches him: out.tearA = cA.anchor === 'bolted' ? (cA.ownRecoil ? Math.min(AA, AB) : AA) / groundTear(cA) : 0 (and the same for B). Add a powers.js check for a bolted loser at unequal presses.

### reasoning
Reproduced. In a standing pin with the power on, the engine says the loser's arm resists with Pl and the winner's surplus spins the forearms (run(s, M, Pl); the page says 'Rex's 2.23 MN surplus over Ollie's arm spins both forearms'). Its slide checks use Pl for both bodies: loserSlides = Rl < Pl and winnerSlides = Rw < Pl. The bolted-rock check, however, uses the winner's whole press for the loser (tearB = AA / groundTear). So within one result the engine loads the loser's feet with 224 kN but his bolts' rock with 2.45 MN. The rock is shown tearing (3.52× its 697 kN) where the force that actually reaches it, 224 kN, is about a third of what tears it. In a stalemate the two presses are equal, so only unequal pins are affected. That needs Things keep their form off, a bolted loser and different strengths, hence minor.

### better_fix
engine.js armWrestle: load each set of bolts with the press that reaches him. In a pin that is the loser's press (min of the two); in a stalemate both are equal.
```js
out.tearA = cA.anchor === 'bolted' ? (cA.ownRecoil ? Math.min(AA, AB) : AA) / groundTear(cA) : 0;
out.tearB = cB.anchor === 'bolted' ? (cB.ownRecoil ? Math.min(AA, AB) : AB) / groundTear(cB) : 0;
```
