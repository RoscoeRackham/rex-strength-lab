### reasoning
Reproduced. In the same arm-wrestle view the verdict says the pin goes through the table, the chip reads 'Pin smashes the table', the tile gives 380× what the top is built for, and the effort box says 'the pin cracks the table'. The 'What happens' item still concludes 'So with the power on a pin never breaks the table'. The README contradicts itself the same way: the 'What changed' bullet and decision 10 say 'A pin never breaks the table with the power on', while 'What moved' gives 'a 200 kJ slam that goes through it'. The PR body repeats the claim, inside the reading-B decision the owner is asked to confirm. What reading B actually guarantees is that the pin's pad load can't tear the table apart at its pegs and pads. The slam is a separate check and still smashes the top.
