Add a switch toggled in place (no reload) on the fireball case, checking a preset's effort against a fresh load. Add a bolted loser's tear status in a standing pin. Add a shoving match with unequal strengths, checking the two recoil figures. Add to powers.js 8g/8i: the ledger with unequal pushes and the tear keys in a standing pin.

### reasoning
Reproduced for the two mutants I applied. Both change what the page or the engine shows, and both survive the pull request's tests. P10, the Collide search counting the absorbed hit energy by default, changes the default set-speed (100 m/s) harm from 'Rex is carried along at 100 m/s and ends up 516 m away' to 'the hit's air blast risks damage 5 m away'. That puts decision 5's effect on the search, and the README's 'now because Rex is carried 516 m' claim, without a check. The set-speed check runs all-out at 300 km/s, where the sound-barrier harm comes first, plus the windows preset, which counts the energy anyway. E16 (the arm tear using his own press) changes tearB for a bolted loser from 3.52 to 0.32. No test reads the tear keys with the power on. P4 (duoKey without the switches): by inspection every Never knocked back check in shots.js reloads the page (sw() and load() both call page.reload()), so a stale in-memory search cache can't be caught. I didn't run the remaining mutants (E25, E32, P6, P7, E24, E27), so the finding stands on the verified ones. These are test-coverage gaps, which this project treats as findings (earlier rounds closed surviving mutants), and they bear on defects confirmed above (the tear in duo-arm-loser-bolts-tear). Minor.
