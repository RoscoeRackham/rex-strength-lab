## title
Collide's steady push drives Rex back with a net 872 kN, but the effort box says nothing in the test does harm, even all-out

## location
rex-strength-lab/src/app.html duoFacts, Collide push branch (lines ~4608-4617: out.harm is set only for rock damage, never for the one pushed being driven back); the shared courtesy sentence 'Nothing in this test does harm, even all-out … A steady push is a slow load, not a blow' (line ~4870)

## evidence
Default state {duoOpen:true,duoAction:'collide',colMode:'push'} at 1280 px. Verdict: 'Ollie wins. He pushes with 890 kN and Rex can hold only 17.7 kN, so Rex is driven back.' Effort box: 'All-out: no cap needed. Nothing the lab checks in this test can do harm here … Courtesy ceiling: none. Nothing in this test does harm, even all-out, with the nearest people and buildings 5 m away. A steady push is a slow load, not a blow.' Engine: standoff(Ollie, Rex) gives outcome 'forceWins', DA 889,644 N, HB 17,652 N, so the net push on Rex is 872 kN, 291 m/s² (about 30 g); over the shove's 2.44 m stroke that is 37.7 m/s. The Wrestle tab's same 890 kN push against a 17.7 kN hold (pushMatch, Rex bolted) sends Ollie off at 37.6 m/s and 109 m, and that tab counts it as harm (its ceiling is 5.47 t). The unlimited case is worse. With {colMode:'push',inf:true} and Rex just standing (the default footing), the verdict says Rex 'is shoved away at just under light speed with unlimited energy', and the same view says 'Nothing in this test does harm with the nearest people and buildings 5 m away.' On pull request 3's page the default was a dead heat at 17.7 kN, so the claim was true there. This pull request made it false. The Two-player effort card also says the checks include 'no one sliding past the nearest people (pushed, …)'.

## expected
The page should not claim no harm when its own verdict has Rex driven back by about 30 g, or flung at light speed. The Known limit ('doesn't follow the motion') should show on the page, not just in the README.

## fix
In duoFacts for the push, when r.ownRecoil && r.outcome === 'forceWins', either set a harm (for example, with an unlimited push: 'Rex is shoved away at just under light speed'), or mark the harm unmodelled and show a gap note instead of 'Nothing … does harm': 'The lab doesn't follow how far the one driven back goes (see Wrestle's shoving match, which does)'. The simplest physics fix is to reuse pushMatchOwn's shoved-off motion for the one driven back.

### better_fix
In engine.js standoff, when ownRecoil && outcome === 'forceWins', add the one pushed's motion with pushMatchOwn's stroke rule:
- d = min(A.strokes.throw, B.strokes.throw)
- net = DA − HB
- t from xOfT
- vB = momentumToV(net·t, mB)
- slideB = slideStop(vB, footing R, B); Infinity when DA is unbounded

In duoFacts' push branch add `out.harm = out.harm || slides(r.slideB);`.

Cheaper copy-only fix: while forceWins with ownRecoil, replace 'Nothing in this test does harm' with a gap note: 'The lab doesn't follow how far the one driven back goes here (Wrestle's shoving match does), so it can't say this push is harmless.'
