### reasoning
Reproduced. In Both at once with Ollie bolted, the verdict says Rex's shove doesn't move Ollie, and Ollie's shove sends Rex back at 21.1 m/s. The only force tile, 'Shove force 299 kN', is r.Fdrive: Rex's comic shove into the bolts, which moves nobody. The shove that produces the motion shown is Ollie's: r.back.Fdrive = 889,644 N, with basis 'real', on a comic-mode page. It is not shown. The 'Comic mode: real muscle does better here' chip is missing even though that shove used real muscle, because basisChip(r) reads only r.basis. On pull request 3's page the 299 kN tile went with 'Nobody moves', so it was accurate. This pull request's new outcome made it misleading.
