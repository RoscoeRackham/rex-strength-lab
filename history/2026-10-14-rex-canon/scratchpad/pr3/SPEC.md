## Decisions (record each in the README section for this pull request and the PR body; the review rounds check them)

1. **On by default**: `DEFAULTS.safePassenger: true`.
2. **Save marker bumped to `saveV: 3`.** An unmarked save, or one marked 2 or lower, loads with Safe passenger on; a save marked 3 keeps the reader's choice. Why 3, not pull request 1's 2: STATUS asks the owner to republish after #31; a save written on a republished page carries saveV 2 with safePassenger false as the old default, and reading it as a choice would leave the new default off for that visitor. Keep the 3,007 kg migration on `savedV < 2` (a {saveV: 2, m: 3007} save keeps its 3,007 kg).
3. **The engine.** Split `MOVES.throw.passenger` into `passengerLaunch` (the launch past the ride limits) and `passengerDrag` (the drag in flight past the held 1.5 g), and `passenger = !o.safePassenger && (launch || drag)`: with the switch off exactly main's boolean; with it on the hazard never fires. Add `MOVES.throw.passengerLanding(r, o)`: `{ absorbed: r.Eimpact (J), J: the landing's momentum (kg·m/s) }` only when Safe passenger is on, the object is a passenger and it lands (not relativistic); null otherwise. It lives in RESTRAINT, not in throwOnce's result, so no result object gains a key. Keep Safe passenger out of `anyPower`.
4. **The landing's split** (the brief's reading; confirm in review): the person comes down unharmed; the landing's momentum still reaches the ground (`landShake` stays and now sets the person throw's ceiling); its energy is taken back (as Soft landing does for his own landings) and is reported for the shimmer (pull request 5). The pulse stays the object's own stop (L / v), as for any landing: Safe passenger spreads the push through them, it doesn't make a slow, gentle stop. Say this on the card and in the README.
5. **No landing blast for a person with the switch on**: its energy is taken back, so `throwBlast`'s landing part is 0 for a passenger with Safe passenger on (guarded for calls with no obj). The crater and quake lines for a very fast person say the energy is taken back. The relativistic branch is left alone (its 'land' is the fireball in the air, not a landing).
6. **Heat is not covered.** Safe passenger covers the push at the launch, the drag in flight and the landing, not heating. Where the page says a person would melt or burn away (Things keep their form off, very fast), it must not also say they land unharmed.
7. **The game of catch with a person**: Safe passenger covers the throw, the flight and the catch (`catchPassengerG` checks the switch first). With it on, a miss's callout says the person comes down unharmed, its energy taken back, its push still shaking the ground. 'hits-ground' stays a failed throw (a harm to the ground, not to the person).
8. **Where the switch sits.** Their powers has no "default powers" heading. Give Safe passenger its own eyebrow (for example "People they throw or catch"), placed with the defaults, and list it in the intro hint and the summary line (#powers-sum) as on by default. The summary's all-defaults line includes it.
9. **The copy.** Wherever the page says the launch "puts no load on them; they float as if falling", "covers only his push, not the air", or "the landing stays real": with the switch on they feel a manageable push, spread through them, at the launch, in the air and when they land; the landing's push still reaches the ground and shakes it; its energy is taken back. With the switch off: today's texts, and the landing stays a fall the lab doesn't judge. The effort box's line "For a person this covers the launch and the air in flight only…" changes accordingly. The effort box credits Safe passenger (offBy) where the same throw with it off presses the person past the ride limits.
10. **Research data.** `comic_passenger_drag_g` in `research/final-presence.json` says "not removed by safe passenger once released": relabel it to the switch-off case under the 14 Oct 2026 ruling (status unchanged), rebuild `src/data.js`.
11. **The powers-off fixture.** `test/powers-off-cases.js` runs 8 planner cases with `safePassenger: true` (`plan|throw|person|inf|…|5`); under this change their passenger hazard (main: the drag at about 39,700 kg-force) becomes never. They are not powers-off cases (Safe passenger is on). In `test/powers.js` part 1, compare those 8 keys with a test-side shim that restores main's passenger rule, and assert that without the shim exactly those 8 differ and nothing else. Don't regenerate the fixture.

## The brief's figures (at 3,000 kg: they differ slightly from the brief's 3,007 kg ones)

A person thrown 30 m at 45°, default dial, the four powers on:
- Safe passenger on: least disruptive 3,342 kg-force, released at 16.4 m/s, goes 30 m, lands at 18.0 m/s (like a 16.5 m fall), 14,600 J taken back. Courtesy ceiling about 27,000 kg-force, set by plaster from the landing.
- Off: least 333 kg-force (3.0 m), ceiling 334 kg-force (the person's g-force).
- All-out: 80.8 m/s, lands 668 m away; with the switch on, the launch, the drag and the landing no longer harm the person.
- All four powers off, Safe passenger on: no ceiling (main: the drag at 39,700 kg-force); plaster may crack only within 1.4 m of the landing.
