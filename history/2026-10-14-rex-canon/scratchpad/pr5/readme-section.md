## The absorption shimmer (BRIEF-canon.md pull request 5)

Canon (CLAUDE.md): "*Where absorbed energy goes* (RT 2, 4.4): back into the store of infinite power they draw on ('a drop in an endless ocean'). It shows: his eyes, feather tips and mouth-glow shimmer in proportion to what he takes in (a flicker for a car crash, a blinding flare for a planet-scale hit). His glow is gently warm, never scalding." The rulings of 14 Oct 2026: "The shimmer: a faint flicker below a car crash; the blinding flare is a dinosaur-killer-scale hit (about 5 × 10²³ J); he shimmers in the growth what-ifs too." The owner confirmed drawing the glow over the art; the art is the owner's, and the image files are unchanged. This pull request builds item 6 of `BRIEF-canon.md`. It is stacked on pull requests 2–4. The engine is commit `3cb9f60`; the page, the cards and the tests follow it.

**What changed.**
- The engine adds up the energy their powers take back in the run a tab shows: `RESTRAINT.takenBack(move, r, o)` returns the total, each part (the air's drag, the ground kept whole, Soft landing, Silent strike, a hit's freed energy, a charger's own stop, a person's landing, a catch, growth) and what each of them takes in (`by.rex`, `by.ollie`). It reads a run's result and options and adds no key to any result, `reach()`, `plan()` or `planNow()`. `EnergyLadder.glow(J, refs)` in `src/ladder.js` turns an energy into the shimmer's level, its word, the glow's strength and the named steps, with its anchors found in the ladder by key.
- A **Shimmer** tile ends the tile row of each tab whose run takes energy back: Throw, Jump (Soft landing), Stomp, Punch and Clap (Silent strike), the Wrestle throw, the game of catch, the partner toss and Collide's charge, and the Weight tab's growth what-if (in its box). It gives the level in words ("a faint flicker", "about a glow", "roughly a blinding flare", "too faint to see"), the energy taken in and what it comes from, the ladder's comparison, and once the note that the glow is gently warm, never scalding (never a heat hazard). In the two-player runs it names who takes each part in. At unlimited strength it says the energy is unbounded, in words: there is no figure. With every power off there is no tile. It is plain text, like the other tiles, so it is what a screen reader hears. Where the game of catch has no tile row (a fireball, a throw that hits the ground), the tile has a row of its own.
- A warm **glow** is drawn over his eyes, feather tips and mouth on the header picture (`dino.jpg`) and the Collide picture (`art-unstoppable.jpg`). Each picture now sits in a box the size of the image, which clips the glow; the layer is hidden from screen readers and clicks. The header's glow follows the run on Rex's own tab; the Collide picture's follows the charge. Both show only Rex's take. Its strength follows the level: none at the floor or below, full at the flare. It shimmers gently where motion is allowed and is static under reduced motion.
- Copy the tally made wrong is fixed. A catch's energy, with the catcher's They take their own recoil on, now goes to the endless ocean (the game of catch and the partner toss had said it ends up as heat in his plush; with the power off they still do). Collide and the fireball no longer say the shimmer isn't drawn yet. The Throw tab's drag-free item (at 100%) names the figure the shimmer counts beside the drag-free one. Collide's carry gives the charger's motion, which goes to his source with his stop, as the tile does. The Wrestle throw's crater-sized landing on Unbreakable footing says its energy is taken back.
- A new card, "The shimmer: what they take in shows", gives what counts, the drag figure, the scale (its figures filled in from the ladder), the floor, the flare's disputed energy and whose shimmer it is. The Game of catch card says a catch's energy is absorbed too.

**Decisions** (the review rounds check them). From the build spec:
1. One tally per run, in the engine, outside every result (`RESTRAINT.takenBack`; not called "shimmer": `VIB.TH.shimmer` and `reach().shimmer` are the water glass's ripple). Its total is 0 with every relevant power off, and no figure in it is NaN.
2. The parts. The air's drag is the share absorbed × what real air would have taken from the same throw (one more flight at the full drag; all of its energy where that flight escapes, orbits, never lands or passes 1% of light speed), not fly()'s tally along the drag-free path, which can be hundreds of times the throw's own energy and is nothing for an orbit (the brief's risk). The same for the Wrestle throw, the toss and each leg of the game of catch. Unbreakable footing: the stomp's energy where real ground would break. Soft landing: the jump's landing. Silent strike: a punch's or clap's energy past Mach 1. A hit's freed energy: Collide's carried hit (unless he means it, a blast preset), and a fireball's motion freed at a catcher with Never knocked back on. A person's landing under Safe passenger. A catch, with the catcher's They take their own recoil on (canon counts a caught object as an incoming hit whose freed energy shows in his shimmer, and absorbs a catch: Q5; not in the brief's list). The growth what-if's energy. Momentum taken back without motion adds nothing.
3. Whose shimmer. Canon gives it to Rex. Until there is a ruling, in the two-player runs the tile names who takes each part in, and the glow on Rex's art shows only Rex's take (STATUS.md, Waiting on).
4. The level: a log scale between canon's two anchors, both ladder rungs found by key: a flicker at a car crash (`energy_corolla_100kmh_kinetic`), the blinding flare at the dinosaur-killer (`energy_chicxulub_impact`, a rough, disputed rung). Named steps a quarter, a half and three quarters of the way, each worded by the ladder's `compare()`; the word for an energy is the nearest step. Below a car crash, a faint flicker, fading to nothing at a floor, Usain Bolt at top speed (`energy_usain_bolt_top_speed`): a design proposal, not canon. Below it the tile says "too faint to see" and no glow is drawn. Past the flare, the flare.
5. The tile: at the end of the row, never between an energy tile and its "Compared with"; the Weight tab's in the growth what-if's box.
6. The glow on the art: a CSS layer at fractions of each picture measured from the image files, beside the pictures in the code (`ART_GLOW`), in a box that clips it, `aria-hidden`, no pointer events, any animation inside the page's reduced-motion guard.
7. Never scalding: the tile's note says once that the glow is gently warm, never a heat hazard.

From the engine stage:

8. The drag is counted once. Where a throw's arrival is taken back too (a catch, a person's landing, a hit, the partner's crater-sized landing), the energy real air would have taken is still in it when it arrives, so the drag part is only what exceeds the arrival's part (in practice nothing). This is what gives the person thrown 30 m its 14.6 kJ, not about twice that.
9. The Wrestle throw's landing is a footing part (not in the spec's list): where the thrown partner lands hard enough that real ground would take a crater, his own Unbreakable footing holds it, and its energy is taken back, as the stomp's is. It is his take, not the thrower's.
10. The charger's own stop (not in the spec's list): in Collide's carried hit the charger stops himself at the end of the squash, and his motion goes to his source with its push back, so it counts as his take (at 0.1% of light speed, Ollie's 135 TJ beside Rex's 135 TJ from the hit). Meant or bounced, it still counts.
11. Whose hit: the freed energy is the one hit's (Rex's when Ollie charges).
12. The jump's footing part is 0: there the power supplies momentum without motion, and Soft landing's part is the landing's.
13. "Roughly" and "about": a step's word takes "roughly" when the rung `compare()` words its energy against is rough (the flare, and the three-quarter step beside the hurricane). Above the flare any finite energy is "roughly a blinding flare"; only an unbounded one is "a blinding flare".
14. The faint flicker is a band, not a step: everything from the floor up to a car crash is "a faint flicker" (canon), and the nearest-step rule applies from the flicker up. So the car thrown all-out (level −0.016) is a faint flicker, and the stomp at a 10⁹ kg dial (level 0.186) is "about a faint glow", its nearest step (the spec's table had said "a flicker" against its own rule; the brief had said "between a flicker and a faint glow").
15. The game of catch is a round trip: leg a's catch is Ollie's, the return leg's Rex's. The page counts the return leg only where Ollie has it to throw back, that is where leg a ends in a clean catch (the round trip's own rule, `catchRoundTrip`); a fireball, a burst ball or a ground hit is leg a alone.
16. The "if he misses" what-if is not in the tile: the tile follows the run (the catch), and the miss keeps its own callout with its figure.
17. Unlimited Collide: at light speed the charge stays pull request 4's "unstoppable" (its hit's energy real), so nothing is taken back there, while 0.1% of light speed glows.
18. Growth: the Weight tab's what-if counts its energy whatever the switches say (it is a what-if, not a power). The growth modes on other tabs turn energy that is already a part (Silent strike, Soft landing, the ground kept whole) into mass, so nothing more is added.

From the page stage:

19. In the two-player runs the tile's level word is Rex's own take ("Rex: about a glow", or "Rex: none"); Ollie's take is named with its energy and its comparison but no glow word, with a note that canon gives the shimmer to Rex and Ollie's is still to be ruled. The level would otherwise claim canon for Ollie.
20. How the glow looks: its strength from `glow()` (0 at the floor, 1 at the flare, linear in the log of the energy between) is drawn at the square root of that, so a car crash's flicker (strength 0.09) still shows (0.3); the glows also grow a little with it (0.8 to 1.2 times). A warm radial glow, screen-blended over the art. With motion allowed each glow brightens and dims a little out of step with its neighbours; under reduced motion it is static.
21. Where the glow goes, measured from the image files (and checked on marked copies and in screenshots at both widths): on `dino.jpg` the two irises, the teeth, and eleven of the crest's orange feather tips from the top of the crest down the back of his neck; on `art-unstoppable.jpg` the two eyes, six glows along the glowing teeth, and ten of the crest's spike tips (just inside each tip, so it lights the spike rather than the bright sky). Off that picture's caption and background spires.
22. Which run drives each glow: the header follows Rex's own tab (the brief left it open), the Collide picture the charge; both use Rex's take only. A tab with nothing taken back (Walk, Lift, Sprint, Pull, Arm, the other Wrestle moves, Collide's steady push) draws none.
23. Wording: the tile says "taken in" (canon: "what he takes in"); the growth what-if's rough energies are given to one figure, as that box gives them. The tile spans its row, since it carries a few lines.
24. The Throw tab's drag-free item keeps its drag-free figure (the push and energy the air would have exchanged along the path flown, as before) and names the shimmer's figure beside it, so the two don't read as one quantity.

**Known limits.**
- The glow sits at fixed fractions of two pictures. It is drawn on the header and the Collide picture only (the Lift and Arm pictures, and Ollie's, have none). At 390 px the header picture is 96 px wide, so an eye's glow is about 7 px across and a feather tip's about 4 px: the tile carries the figures.
- The tile's level is a step on a log scale whose top, the dinosaur-killer's energy, is disputed (one older estimate is about 33 times higher). Only the flare and the three-quarter step say "roughly" (the ladder's own rule, decision 13); the quarter and half steps' energies hang on the flare too, so they are less firm than "about" suggests. The floor is the lab's proposal.
- The drag the shimmer counts is what real air would have taken; where the throw lands, its energy isn't lost to the world (the object lands with it). The drag-free figure the Throw tab also gives is the drag along the path flown. Which one canon's shimmer means is for the owners (decision 2).
- The "if he misses" what-if is not in the tile (decision 16). At light speed Collide's hit is real, so no shimmer shows (decision 17).
- In forced-colours mode the browser may drop the glow (it is decoration; the tile still reads).
- Ollie's shimmer is not drawn and has no level word: canon doesn't say whether he shows what he takes in (decision 19).
