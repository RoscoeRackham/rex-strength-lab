Pull request 5 of BRIEF-canon.md, engine: the energy taken back and the shimmer's level

RESTRAINT.takenBack(move, r, o) adds up the energy their powers take back in the
run a tab shows, by part (drag, footing, soft landing, silent strike, a hit's
freed energy, the charger's own stop, a passenger's landing, a catch, growth)
and by who takes it in (Rex or Ollie). The drag part is the share absorbed of
what real air would take from the same throw, counted once where the arrival
is taken back too. It adds no key to any result; unlimited runs are reported
as unbounded, and no figure is NaN.

EnergyLadder.glow(J, refs) maps that energy to the shimmer's level on a log
scale from a car crash (a flicker) to the dinosaur-killer (the flare), with a
faint flicker fading out at Usain Bolt's energy; the anchors are ladder rungs
found by key, and the steps are worded with compare().
